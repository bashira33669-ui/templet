Admission Policy & Criteria

Admissions at Oriental Degree College are open to all eligible candidates based on merit and previous academic records. We offer admissions in Associate Degree (AD) programs and Bachelor of Education (B.Ed) programs.

Required Documents

Applicants are required to submit copies of their previous transcripts/degrees, CNIC or B-Form, passport-sized photographs, and a completed admission form before the closing date.

How to Apply

You can visit our college campus office in Drosh, Lower Chitral, to collect the admission prospectus and application form, or contact our administration department for further assistance regarding fee structures and deadlines.
