Our Offered Courses & Programs

AD Programs (Associate Degree)

Our AD Programs are designed to equip students with specialized knowledge and practical skills. We offer major departments including English, Botany, Islamiyat, and Computer Science with highly qualified faculty and well-equipped labs.

B.Ed Programs (Bachelor of Education)

The B.Ed program follows a comprehensive scheme of studies covering Educational Psychology, Curriculum Development, Research Methods, School Management, and hands-on Practice Teaching sessions.

Facilities & Practical Learning

All our courses are supported by modern computer labs, physics laboratories, a rich library, and a conducive environment to foster complete academic and professional growth.
